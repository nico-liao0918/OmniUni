Elemental User

Part 1

In a Time apart of the Universe. There is a world or planet that is about to begin their very own power system.

This world is part of much larger universe - The Zharian Universe. Let's take a look.

Enjoy the story.


Hello, I am Narrator. And what you are about to hear is a story on Prime 16, Earth 40. A world of Elemental Users, well, about to be one.

In the Beginning. The world has no powers yet, but soon, its gonna happen.

Now, we see a man in his early 30s in the Philippines named James, and as well as a man, also in his early 30s, from Japan whose name is Taro.


Our story begins on the day of January 15th, of the year of 2025.

In the Philippines.:

James is preparing to fly to Tokyo, Japan to have a vacation and he already booked a flight, have a visa, and all required documents with him. James has also, gotten or booked his activities on Klook early on before his Flight at January 20th of 2025.

Meanwhile in Japan.:

Taro is preparing for his trip to Tokyo via his Car, he also booked his activities on Klook. Taro have his documents and money for travel and tollgate and other purchases along his trip to Tokyo from his home, his apartment in Osaka.


After some time. Both Taro and James have done what they need or ought to do and set forth on thier adventure to Tokyo.
